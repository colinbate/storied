The useful bits
stay connected.

A chat thread is fine until you need something from six months ago. Storied connects each conversation to the relevant books, sessions, and people.

01

Discussions you can find again

Start focused threads, write in Markdown, mention fellow readers, hide spoilers, and find an idea again long after the meeting ends.

02

Meetings from plan to recap

Share time and place, collect RSVPs, message attendees, build an agenda, run the meeting, record attendance, gather feedback, and publish a recap.

24 AUG✓ Attending
03

A shared library with club history

Browse books, authors, series, genres, and club classifications. See when a title was featured or discussed, who read it, and where members can find a copy.

+123
04

Room for different reading choices

Members can record what they plan to read, are reading, finished, or left unfinished for each session. Shared picks and individual choices can live side by side.

You run it.
You own the data.

Storied is open-source software that you run in your own Cloudflare account. The reference setup pairs the private member space with a separate public club site. A private-only setup also works, though a few public integration points still need a manual pass.

  • 01 SvelteKit application at the edge
  • 02 D1 database and migrations
  • 03 R2 uploads, Workers, Queues, and email
  • 04 Open source, inspectable, and adaptable

A few deliberate choices.

◎

Discussion stays focused

No algorithmic feed or engagement scores. Your people can simply talk about books.

◐

Reading comes first

Responsive layouts, dark mode, and an optional OpenDyslexic typeface put readers first.

◇

Change what you need

Inspect the code, adapt the data model, and fit Storied to the way your club already runs.

A few things
on the list.

Storied already handles the day-to-day work of running my book club. These additions would make it easier to adapt to clubs with different reading and meeting formats.

01

Member-led library curation

Let members suggest genres, classifications, availability links, and metadata fixes, with a review queue for moderators. Administrators can maintain these details today.

02

Configurable session vocabulary

Let each club replace Starter, Featured, Discussed, and Off-Theme Mentions with terms that fit its format, and identify a session's primary read when it has one.

03

Book/theme voting

Let members suggest and vote on future reads, then carry the selected book into a session without entering it again.
