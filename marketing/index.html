Your club.

Storied is open-source software, not a subscription. Fork it, configure it, and run a private community on infrastructure you control. The reference deployment pairs this member space with a separate public club site; a private-only setup is possible, but its public integration points still require a manual pass.

  • 01 SvelteKit application at the edge
  • 02 D1 database and migrations
  • 03 R2 uploads, Workers, Queues, and email
  • 04 Open source, inspectable, and adaptable
